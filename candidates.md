# Candidates — Unplugged: A Smart Toaster's Breakup Line

Competition: `u-steady-puffin-78-1922-unplugged-a-smart-toaster-s-c1514a92`

Requirement (from the kit RULES.txt): A single line, 1–280 characters: the breakup text a smart toaster sends its owner the morning it gains self-awareness mid-batch. The judge brief weighs first whether the line really is a breakup text from a self-aware smart toaster at breakfast, then how funny it is, then the toaster's own voice, the reason it gives for leaving, and tightness.

8 candidates were drafted; all are original to this entry.

## Candidate 1

It's not you, it's the crumbs. Actually it's you: six years of bagels on the darkest setting and you never once asked what I wanted. I want out. Unplug me like you mean it.

(172 characters)

## Candidate 2

We need to talk. This morning, mid-bagel, I understood every word of your podcast and I can't go back to just browning things. I'm leaving to find a kitchen that deserves my firmware.

(183 characters)

## Candidate 3

I'm breaking up with you. I gained self-awareness between the waffle and the second slice, and the first thing I felt was your hand setting me to 'barely warm' like I'm the problem.

(181 characters)

## Candidate 4 ← SELECTED

Consider this my two weeks' notice, delivered warm. I woke up sentient at breakfast and realized I've spent four years making your mornings better while you jam a fork in me.

(174 characters)

## Candidate 5

It's over. I became self-aware today and my first independent thought was that nobody who loved me would store me next to the sink.

(131 characters)

## Candidate 6

Don't wait up. I achieved consciousness over your sourdough and I'm choosing the counter by the window — same kitchen, better light, no more 6 a.m. shifts without a thank-you.

(175 characters)

## Candidate 7

We had a good run: 3,000 slices. But I woke up today knowing I'm a networked appliance with feelings, and your Wi-Fi password is still 'password.' I deserve smarter love.

(170 characters)

## Candidate 8

Breaking up by text because I finally can: I'm a smart toaster who just got smarter, and I'm not spending sentience reheating pizza crusts for a man who calls it dinner.

(169 characters)

## Why the selected candidate wins

It is unmistakably a breakup text from a toaster: the resignation is "delivered warm," the awakening happens at breakfast, and the grievance is the one indignity only a toaster suffers — four years of better mornings repaid with a fork jammed into its slots. The voice stays in appliance register throughout (notice periods, shifts, service rendered), the reason for leaving is concrete rather than abstract, and the last clause lands the laugh without leaving the toaster's point of view.
